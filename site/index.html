What each check means

Freshness

The newest record must be younger than the feed's SLA: 2 hours for earthquakes, 54 hours for Wikipedia, 10 days for Chicago, which publishes about a week behind.

Volume

Row count against a robust baseline (median and MAD of recent healthy runs; the same weekday for daily feeds). Fails beyond 4.5 deviations.

Schema

A removed column or changed type fails; a new column warns. A column that goes entirely blank is reported by completeness instead.

Completeness

Each column's null rate against its usual level. A jump of 10 points warns, 30 points fails.

Uniqueness

Record ids must not repeat within a batch.

Distribution

Population stability index of magnitudes, depths, views, hours and categories against the last two weeks. Above 0.2 warns: the world changes, so drift is a signal, not an outage.

Failing checks open a GitHub issue automatically and close it when the check passes again. Code, history and incident log on GitHub.

Data: USGS, Wikimedia, City of Chicago. Faded ticks are history replayed from each feed's archive when monitoring began.
